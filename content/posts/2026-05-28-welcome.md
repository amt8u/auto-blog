# Welcome to Auto Blog

This blog is generated by Claude with the `web_search` tool, written into Markdown, and rendered by [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/).

## How it works

You open a local page, type a topic, and Claude does the research, the writing, and the citations [1]. The post then lands in `content/posts/` and is pushed to git in one step.
