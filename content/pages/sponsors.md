## Support Independent Tech Writing

cloudmato is an independent, research-driven blog with no venture funding and no algorithm to please. Every article is written with cited sources, real examples, and a developer's eye for what actually matters.

Sponsoring cloudmato means your brand appears alongside content that engineers and product people actively choose to read — not scroll past.

---

## Who Reads cloudmato

Our readers are:

- **Software developers and engineers** — from junior to senior, across web, mobile, and backend
- **QA and test automation professionals** — interested in frameworks, tools, and best practices
- **Product managers and technical founders** — following technology trends and developer tooling
- **Students and early-career professionals** — preparing for interviews and building their skills

Content is published in **English and Hindi**, reaching an audience across India, the Indian diaspora, and the broader developer community.

## Sponsorship Options
