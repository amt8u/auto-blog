## Contact

cloudmato.com is run by a small team that researches and writes every article on this site. We'd like to hear from you.

### Get in touch

**Email:** [cloudmato@gmail.com](mailto:cloudmato@gmail.com)

Reach out if you'd like to:

- Suggest a topic for a future article
- Report an error, outdated information, or broken link
- Ask a question about something covered on the site

We read every email and try to respond within a few days.

### Found an error?

If something in an article is incorrect or out of date, please include the article URL and a short description of the issue — we'll review and correct it.
