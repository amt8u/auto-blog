## Engagement Types

### Product Development
Full-stack web and mobile product builds for early-stage companies and teams launching new internal tools. Typically 2–6 month engagements.

### Quality Engineering Retainer
Ongoing QA and test automation support embedded into your sprint cycle. Monthly retainer, no long-term lock-in.

### One-Time Audit
Architecture review, security posture assessment, or test coverage gap analysis delivered as a structured report with an actionable remediation plan. Fixed scope, fixed price.

### Team Augmentation
Senior engineering capacity for specific projects or deadlines. Works alongside your existing team without the overhead of a new hire.
