## Performance Engineering

### Know Your Limits Before Your Users Find Them

A product that works for 100 users and falls over at 10,000 is a product with an invisible expiry date. We load-test, profile, and benchmark so that scaling events become planned milestones — not incidents.

**What this solves:**
- Unpredictable behaviour under traffic spikes
- Database queries that degrade at production data volumes
- Infrastructure sizing guesswork before major launches

**What you get:**
- Load and stress testing with k6, Locust, or JMeter
- Frontend performance audits (Core Web Vitals, Time to Interactive)
- Bottleneck identification across frontend, API, and database layers
- Capacity planning reports with actionable recommendations
